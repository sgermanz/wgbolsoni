1. PROFILE SUMMARY AND ENTREPRENEURIAL HISTORY

Wilton Gerson Bolsoni is an industrialist, an entrepreneur in foundational industries and a senior corporate advisor (Business Advisor) whose continuous career spans more than five decades in domestic and international markets. Having begun his commercial activities in 1976, he established his reputation as a founder, majority shareholder and manager of private organizational complexes and investment holding companies, distinguishing himself through versatility in leading highly regulated and technically complex economic sectors.

2. BUSINESS SECTORS AND INDUSTRIAL ACTIVITIES

Wilton Gerson Bolsoni's presence in Brazil's industrial ecosystem encompasses structuring, governance and executive management across multiple foundational sectors and large-scale supply chains:
• Agribusiness and Meat Processing Operations (Present Day): He works in the agro-industrial processing of animal and plant protein, actively pursuing the development, manufacture and supply of innovative ready-to-eat meat products. His governance extends to modern distribution chains and the addition of technological value in the food sector.
• Advanced Logistics and 4PL Infrastructure: He acts as the strategic leader behind the Krystallos Dock Logistic & 4PL S.A. project, an operation focused on high-capacity intralogistics. The project involves developing and implementing automated systems composed of intelligent stacker cranes operating in cold-storage chambers under extreme thermal conditions down to -40ºC at heights of 25 meters.
• Engineering and Foundational Industry: A history of founding and governing corporations dedicated to complex industrial assembly in electrical engineering and heavy mechanical engineering, as well as industrial processing operations in paper, pulp and alcohol-based chemistry.
• Petrochemicals and Distribution: Activities in establishing and directing plastic packaging factories and in the logistics of distributing fuels and petroleum derivatives.
• Real Estate: Conception and development of urban and rural projects and structured real estate development ventures.
